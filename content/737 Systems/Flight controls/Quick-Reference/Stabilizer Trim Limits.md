# Stabilizer Trim Limits
🔗 [[🏠 Home]] | [[Stabilizer and Trim]]

| Mode | Minimum (units) | Maximum (units) |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| Main electric — flaps extended | 0.05 | 14.5 |
| Main electric — flaps retracted | 3.95 | 14.5 |
| Autopilot trim | 0.05 | 14.5 |
| Manual trim | -0.20 | 16.9 |

> [!note]
> Green band on STAB TRIM indicator = takeoff trim range.
> Manual trim wheel has the widest authority — use in runaway or electric failure.
