## Notes on Caution Lights

> [!info] LOW PRESS light — when it will NOT illuminate
> The FLT CTRL LOW PRESSURE light will **not** illuminate when the related FLT CONTROL switch is in STBY RUD position and the standby rudder shutoff valve is open (standby system active).

> [!info] STBY RUD ON — auto activation
> Illuminates automatically during **takeoff and landing** (standby rudder auto-activates) and on **FFM detection** of force fight between Hyd A and B actuators.

> [!info] MACH TRIM FAIL / SPEED TRIM FAIL on recall
> Both of these indicate a **single FCC (Flight Control Computer) failure** when recalled. They share the same FCC — one failure illuminates both on recall.
