## Flight Control Hydraulic Packages

> [!info]
> There are **two** Flight Control Hydraulic Packages — one for Hyd A and one for Hyd B.
> Located in the **front section of the main wheel well**.
>
> - Controlled by FLIGHT CONTROL and SPOILER switches A and B (operate shutoff valves)
> - Control and monitor hydraulic pressure to **primary flight controls** and **flight spoilers**
> - Some aircraft have an additional **compensator cartridge** maintaining 40–70 psi when systems are depressurised (gust protection)
