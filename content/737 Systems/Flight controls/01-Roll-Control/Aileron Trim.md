# Aileron Trim
🔗 [[Roll Control|← Roll Control]]

---

## Operation

- **Dual switches** on glareshield — both must be pushed simultaneously
- Electrically drives the **[[Aileron Feel and Centering Unit|Aileron Trim Actuator]]** to redefine the neutral position
- Control wheel physically rotates to reflect trim change
- **1 trim unit = 6° of control wheel rotation**

---

## Autopilot Interaction

> [!danger] PROHIBITED with A/P engaged
> Use of aileron trim with A/P engaged is **prohibited**.
> - A/P actuator overpowers the trim input to maintain desired bank — no roll change is apparent
> - On A/P disconnect: the out-of-trim condition is unmasked → **abrupt aileron swing may occur**
