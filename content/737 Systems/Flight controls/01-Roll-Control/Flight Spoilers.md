# Flight Spoilers
🔗 [[Roll Control|← Roll Control]]

> [!abstract]
> 8 flight spoilers (4 per wing) serve dual roles: roll augmentation and in-flight speed brakes. Commanded via the [[Spoiler Mixer|Spoiler Mixer]] which combines roll inputs from F/O wheel and speedbrake lever position.

---

## Panel Numbering, Type & Hydraulic Power

| Panel | Wing side | Type | Hydraulic |
|-------|-----------|------|-----------|
| 1 | Left | **Ground spoiler** | Hyd A |
| 2, 3 | Left | Flight spoiler | Hyd A |
| 4, 5 | Left | Flight spoiler | Hyd B |
| 6 | Left | **Ground spoiler** | Hyd A |
| 7 | Right | **Ground spoiler** | Hyd A |
| 8, 9 | Right | Flight spoiler | Hyd B |
| 10, 11 | Right | Flight spoiler | Hyd A |
| 12 | Right | **Ground spoiler** | Hyd A |

> [!info] Spoiler extension sequence
> **Panels 4+5 and 8+9 (inner) extend first**, then outer panels as wheel displacement increases.

---

## Roll Mode

- F/O wheel → [[Spoiler Control Shaft|Spoiler Control Shaft]] → [[Spoiler Mixer|Spoiler Mixer]] → spoiler actuators
- Spoilers activate when wheel displaced > **10°** (= **1.6 trim units**)
- Rise on the **up-aileron wing**; fair on the down-aileron wing
- Increase roll rate — especially effective at high airspeeds

---

## Speed Brake Mode

- All flight spoilers rise **symmetrically** when speed brake lever deployed
- [[Speed Brakes|Speed brake lever]] → Spoiler Mixer → combined roll + speedbrake signal

> [!danger] Flight detent limit
> Do **NOT** move speed brake lever beyond FLIGHT DETENT in flight.
> Severe buffeting and structural loads result.

---

## Redundancy

| Hyd B lost | Hyd A lost |
|-----------|-----------|
| Panels 4,5,8,9 inoperative | Panels 2,3,10,11 inoperative |
| Panels 2,3,10,11 remain (Hyd A) | Panels 4,5,8,9 remain (Hyd B) |
| Symmetric roll augmentation maintained | Symmetric roll augmentation maintained |
