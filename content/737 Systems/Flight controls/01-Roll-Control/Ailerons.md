# Ailerons
🔗 [[Roll Control|← Roll Control]]

> [!abstract]
> Primary roll surface powered by Hyd A and B. Either system is sufficient. Manual reversion available with a 3° dead band.

---

## Control Paths

> [!note] CPT control path
> CPT control wheel → **Aileron Input Shaft** → **[[Aileron Feel and Centering Unit|Feel & Centering Unit]]** → **[[Aileron PCU and Pogos|Aileron PCUs]]** → **[[Aileron Body Quadrants|Aileron Body Quadrants]]** → Aileron surface

> [!note] F/O control path
> F/O control wheel → **[[Spoiler Control Shaft|Spoiler Control Shaft]]** → **[[Spoiler Mixer|Spoiler Mixer]]** → Spoiler PCUs → Flight Spoilers

- Both control wheels interconnected via cables through the **[[Transfer Mechanism|Aileron Transfer Mechanism]]**
- FLT CONTROL switches A & B: shutoff valves in hydraulic packages

---

## Hydraulic Power

| PCU | Hydraulic |
|-----|-----------|
| Left aileron PCU | Hyd B |
| Right aileron PCU | Hyd A |

Either PCU alone can operate the ailerons.

---

## Manual Reversion

- Possible with total hydraulic failure
- CPT wheel must turn > **3°** (dead band) before input cranks hit mechanical stops and move PCU housing
- PCU housing movement drives [[Aileron Body Quadrants|Aileron Body Quadrants]] to commanded position
- Forces significantly higher than normal
