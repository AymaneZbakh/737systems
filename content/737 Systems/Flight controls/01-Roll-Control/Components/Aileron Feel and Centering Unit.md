# Aileron Feel & Centering Unit
🔗 [[Roll Control|← Roll Control]]

> [!abstract]
> Provides artificial feel forces to the CPT control wheel. Feel force is generated by springs that stretch as the Aileron Input Shaft turns from neutral. Neutral position can be redefined by the Aileron Trim Actuator.

---

## How Feel is Generated

- A set of **springs** stretch as the Aileron Input Shaft rotates from neutral
- The further the wheel is displaced, the higher the feel force
- This simulates aerodynamic feedback that would exist in an unpowered system

---

## Trim Actuator

- **Aileron Trim Actuator** is co-located with the Feel & Centering Unit
- Electrically repositions the **neutral position** of the unit
- Triggered by aileron trim switches (dual, simultaneous)
- 1 trim unit = 6° of control wheel rotation

> [!danger]
> If trim is applied with A/P engaged, the A/P overpowers the change. On A/P disconnect, the stored out-of-trim causes an **abrupt aileron swing**. See [[Aileron Trim]].
