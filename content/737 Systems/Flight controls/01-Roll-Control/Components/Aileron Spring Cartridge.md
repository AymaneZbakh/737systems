# Aileron Spring Cartridge
🔗 [[Roll Control|← Roll Control]]

> [!abstract]
> Interconnects the spoiler controls with the aileron controls. Similar purpose to the Aileron Transfer Mechanism — in case of a jam in one system, the Spring Cartridge separates the systems to isolate the jam.

---

## Function

- Mechanically links the **Spoiler Mixer** output to the **Aileron control system**
- Allows normal operation: F/O inputs are sequenced through the aileron path
  `F/O wheel → Spoiler Control Shaft → Aileron Spring Cartridge → Spoilers`
- On jam: spring cartridge **compresses/extends** to separate the two systems

---

## Jam Isolation

> [!info]
> If a jam occurs in the aileron system, the Spring Cartridge prevents the jam from blocking spoiler operation (and vice versa).
> The systems separate, each maintaining independent function via their respective hydraulic paths.

---

## Location

Located in the **main wheel well**, adjacent to the Spoiler Mixer.
