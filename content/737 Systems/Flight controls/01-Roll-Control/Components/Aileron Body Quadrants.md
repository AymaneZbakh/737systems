# Aileron Body Quadrants
🔗 [[Roll Control|← Roll Control]]

> [!abstract]
> Transfer control output from the Aileron PCUs to the ailerons through a set of cables.

---

## Function

- Receive commanded position from Aileron PCU actuators
- Transfer via cables to aileron surfaces (wing)
- Two quadrants — if a jam occurs in one quadrant, **the other quadrant can break away** to maintain aileron movement

---

## Jam Protection

> [!info]
> The break-away feature of the Aileron Body Quadrants prevents a jammed quadrant from blocking the other side, maintaining at least partial aileron authority.
