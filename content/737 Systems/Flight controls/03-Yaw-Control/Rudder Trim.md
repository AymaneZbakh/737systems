# Rudder Trim
🔗 [[Yaw Control|← Yaw Control]]

> [!abstract]
> Electrically repositions the Rudder Feel & Centering Unit to redefine the rudder neutral position. When trimming, rudder pedals move proportionately.

---

## Operation

- Control: **RUDDER trim knob** on aft electronic panel
- Powered by **XFR Bus #2** (Transfer Bus #2)
- Electrically drives the **Rudder Feel & Centering Unit** to a new neutral position
- Rudder pedals **physically move** to reflect trim (unlike aileron trim)

---

## Use Cases

- Correct for asymmetric thrust (e.g. engine inoperative)
- Correct for crosswind during extended cruise
- Reduce sustained pedal force

> [!tip]
> Unlike aileron trim, rudder pedals **do move** when rudder trim is applied — both main and standby yaw damper inputs can also be overridden by rudder trim inputs.
