# Elevator Tab Control System
🔗 [[Pitch Control|← Pitch Control]]

> [!abstract]
> The elevator tab changes function depending on flap position — acting as either a Balance Tab (reducing control forces) or an Anti-balance Tab (increasing nose-up authority).

---

## Tab Function by Flap Position

| Flap Position | Tab Type | Tab Behaviour | Effect |
|---------------|----------|---------------|--------|
| Flaps **UP** | **Balance Tab** | Moves **0.75°** opposite to elevator | Reduces pitch control forces |
| Flaps **NOT UP** | **Anti-balance Tab** | Moves **0.5°** in the same direction | Increases nose-up control authority |

---

## Why Anti-balance with Flaps?

> [!info]
> When flaps are extended (approach/landing), the aerodynamic download on the tailplane increases.
> The anti-balance tab moving with the elevator **increases nose-up pitch authority**, counteracting the increased download.
> This gives the pilot better flare authority during landing.
