# Stabiliser Alignment Tabs
🔗 [[Pitch Control|← Pitch Control]]

> [!abstract]
> Three external alignment tabs on the left side of the fuselage provide a visual reference for stabiliser position — showing maximum leading edge up, neutral, and maximum leading edge down positions.

---

## Full Stabiliser Travel

**17.1°** total movement

---

## Alignment Tab Reference

| Tab | Position | Leading Edge | Stab Trim Units |
|-----|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| Upper | Max leading edge **up** (nose **down**) | 4.2° up | **0 units** |
| Middle | **Neutral** | 0° | **4 units** |
| Lower | Max leading edge **down** (nose **up**) | 12.9° down | **17 units** |

---

## STAB TRIM Indicator (Cockpit)

- Scale shows **0 (top/APL NOSE DOWN)** to **17 (bottom/APL NOSE UP)**
- Green band = **takeoff trim range** (approximately 4–9 units depending on CG/weight)
