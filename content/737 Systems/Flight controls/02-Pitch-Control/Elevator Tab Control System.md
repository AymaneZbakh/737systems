# Elevator Tab Control System
🔗 [[Pitch Control|← Pitch Control]]

> [!abstract]
> The elevator tab changes function depending on flap position. With flaps UP it acts as a balance tab (reduces control forces). With flaps NOT UP it acts as an anti-balance tab (increases nose-up authority).

---

## Tab Function by Configuration

| Flap Position | Tab Type | Movement | Effect |
|---------------|----------|----------|--------|
| Flaps **UP** | **Balance tab** | 0.75° opposite per 1° elevator | Reduces elevator hinge moment → lower column forces |
| Flaps **NOT UP** | **Anti-balance tab** | 0.5° same direction per 1° elevator | Increases nose-up control — important for approach/landing |

---

## Why Anti-Balance on Approach?

> [!info]
> With flaps extended, the aerodynamic nose-down moment increases significantly. The anti-balance tab moves in the same direction as the elevator, increasing the aerodynamic nose-up authority and ensuring adequate pitch-up control during landing flare.
