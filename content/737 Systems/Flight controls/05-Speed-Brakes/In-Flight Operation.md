# In-Flight Speed Brake Operation
🔗 [[Speed Brakes|← Speed Brakes]]

---

## Deployment

- All **flight spoilers** (2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 11) rise symmetrically
- Ground spoilers (1, 6, 7, 12) do **not** deploy in flight
- Speed brake lever positions: ARMED through FLIGHT DETENT

---

## Roll Interaction

> [!important]
> Speed brakes deployed + roll input = **greatly increased roll rate**
> - Spoilers on the up-aileron wing rise even higher (speed brake + roll command)
> - Spoilers on the down-aileron wing remain deployed or rise less
> - Intermediate speed brake position during turns significantly increases roll response — be prepared

---

## Flight Detent Limit

- **FLIGHT DETENT = maximum in-flight position**
- Beyond FLIGHT DETENT (UP): **prohibited** — severe airframe buffeting and structural loads
- SFP aircraft: lever stop prevents going beyond FLIGHT DETENT with flaps up

---

## Load Alleviation

- Some aircraft versions: auto-retract to **50%** at high gross weight / high airspeed
- Prevents structural overload
- Manual override with increased lever force

---

## SPD BRK EXTENDED Light (Amber)

> [!warning]
> Illuminates when:
> - Speed brake lever is **beyond ARMED position (7.5°)**, AND
> - **TE Flaps > 10°**, OR **RA < 800 ft**
>
> Retract speed brakes immediately if this light illuminates on approach.
