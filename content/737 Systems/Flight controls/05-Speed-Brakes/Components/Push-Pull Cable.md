# Push-Pull Cable
🔗 [[Speed Brakes|← Speed Brakes]]

> [!abstract]
> Connected to the Right Main Gear strut. Supplies the air/ground mechanical command to the Ground Spoiler Interlock Valve. When the Right MLG strut compresses on landing, this cable mechanically opens the Interlock Valve.

---

## Function

| State | Cable Position | Interlock Valve |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| In flight (gear extended, no weight) | Extended / neutral | Closed |
| Weight on Right MLG (strut compressed) | Pushed | **Opens** → allows Hyd A to ground spoilers |

---

## Why Right Main Gear?

- The Right MLG strut is the sensing point for the ground spoiler system
- This is independent of the WOW (Weight on Wheels) electrical switches used by other systems
- Purely **mechanical** — no electrical signal or relay required

---

## Failure Implication

> [!warning]
> If push-pull cable is disconnected or fails:
> - Ground Spoiler Interlock Valve remains closed on landing
> - Ground spoilers will not deploy automatically
> - Flight spoilers still deploy via wheel spin-up / auto speedbrake module
