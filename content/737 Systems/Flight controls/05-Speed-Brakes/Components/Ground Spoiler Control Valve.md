# Ground Spoiler Control Valve
🔗 [[Speed Brakes|← Speed Brakes]]

> [!abstract]
> Receives mechanical inputs from the Spoiler Mixer to open when the Speedbrake Lever is moved beyond FLIGHT DETENT. When open, sends Hyd A pressure to the Ground Spoiler Interlock Valve.

---

## Function

| Condition | Valve State | Result |
|-----------|------------|--------|
| Speedbrake lever ≤ FLIGHT DETENT | **Closed** | No pressure to ground spoilers |
| Speedbrake lever > FLIGHT DETENT | **Open** | Hyd A pressure flows to Ground Spoiler Interlock Valve |

---

## Mechanical Link

- Receives a **mechanical input from the Spoiler Mixer** (via Ground Spoiler Control Valve mechanical link on Spoiler Control Shaft)
- Purely mechanical actuation — no electrical signal required

---

## Location

Located in the **main wheel well**, connected to the Spoiler Control Shaft assembly.
