# 🔧 CAB/UTIL Switch

> [!abstract] Overview
> The CAB/UTIL switch controls a broad set of cabin utility systems, and cycling it is also the standard method for attempting to reset an automatically shed bus.

## 📋 Systems That Lose Power When OFF

| System |
|---|
| All galley busses |
| Recirculation fans |
| Logo lights |
| Potable water compressor |
| Door area, drain mast and lavatory water heaters |
| LED cabin lights (Sky Interior) |

> [!tip] Resetting shed buses
> Cycling the CAB/UTIL switch is the standard technique to attempt resetting a bus shed by [[Auto-Load-Shedding]].
