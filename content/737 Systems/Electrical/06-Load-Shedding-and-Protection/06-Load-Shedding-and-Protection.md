# ⛽ 06 — Load Shedding & Protection

> [!abstract] Overview
> When electrical current threatens to exceed limits, the [[BPCU]] automatically removes ("sheds") non-essential loads — galley and main buses first — to protect the system. Cabin switches control which secondary systems can be shed manually.

## 🔧 Components

| Component | Summary |
|---|---|
| [[Auto-Load-Shedding]] | The 3 automatic load-shed types and their bus-by-bus effects |
| [[CAB-UTIL-Switch]] | Cabin/utility loads; can reset auto-shed buses |
| [[IFE-PASS-SEAT-Switch]] | Passenger seat & IFE power |

> [!tip] Resetting a shed bus
> You can try to **reset** automatically shed buses by cycling the [[CAB-UTIL-Switch|CAB/UTIL switch]].
