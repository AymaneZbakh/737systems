# 🔧 IDG (Integrated Drive Generator)

> [!abstract] Overview
> The IDG converts variable engine speed into a constant drive speed so its internal brushless AC generator can output stable 400Hz power. There are **two**, one per engine.

## 📋 Key Facts

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Output | 90 KVA |
| Internal generator speed | Constant 24,000 RPM |
| Mounting | Accessory gearbox, beneath the starter motor (left front side of engine) |
| Oil functions | Cools, lubricates, and operates the IDG |
| Oil cooling | Two heat exchangers: air/oil and fuel/oil |
| Oil quantity markings | **Two** markings — accounts for wing dihedral / differing IDG tilt |

## 💡 DRIVE Light

> [!warning] DRIVE Light — illuminates on IDG low oil pressure
> Possible causes:
>
> | Cause | Detail |
> |---|---|
> | Underfrequency | Engine shutdown, manual drive disconnect, or drive failure |
> | IDG low oil pressure | Direct pressure fault |
> | Auto disconnect | High IDG oil temperature — **182°C / 363°F** |

## 🔌 DISCONNECT Switch

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Type | Electro-mechanical disconnect system |
| Disconnect condition | Start lever in **IDLE** *and* electrical power available |
| Reconnection | **Ground only**, by maintenance personnel |
| Guarding | Disconnect switches are guarded behind covers to prevent accidental use |
