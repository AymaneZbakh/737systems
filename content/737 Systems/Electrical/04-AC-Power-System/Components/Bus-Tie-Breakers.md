# 🔧 Bus Tie Breakers (BTB)

> [!abstract] Overview
> The Bus Tie Breakers physically link AC Transfer Bus 1 and AC Transfer Bus 2, and are entirely governed by the [[BPCU]].

## 📋 Key Facts

| Fact |
|---|
| Controlled by the **[[BPCU]]** |
| BPCU ensures **no paralleling** of the AC system |
| BPCU also monitors the External Power Contactor and controls the [[Auto-Load-Shedding\|load shed function]] |

> [!warning] Hard rule
> It is **not possible** to power one Transfer Bus from the APU and the other from Ground Power — this would contradict the no-paralleling rule.
