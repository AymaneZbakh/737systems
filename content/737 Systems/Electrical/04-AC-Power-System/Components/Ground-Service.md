# 🔧 Ground Service

> [!abstract] Overview
> Ground Service Buses allow servicing the cabin (e.g., cabin lights) without powering up the flight deck — keeping it "cold and dark."

## 📋 Key Facts

| Fact |
|---|
| Ground Service Buses are powered either from the **on-side AC Transfer Bus**, or **directly from the Ground Power source** |
| The **Ground Service** switch is located at the **forward attendant station** |
| Pushing it powers **Ground Service Bus 1 and 2** directly from ground power |
| Allows cabin servicing without powering up the flight deck |
