# 🔧 Cross Bus Tie Relay (CBTR)

> [!abstract] Overview
> The CBTR parallels the three [[Transformer-Rectifier|Transformer Rectifiers]] so they share DC load, and opens to isolate the DC system during critical approach phases or when commanded by the crew.

## 📋 Key Facts

| State | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Normally closed** | Three TRs connected in parallel, sharing the load |
| **Opens** | G/S capture (F/D or A/P approach) — isolates DC busses, preventing a single failure from affecting both receivers |
| **Opens** | [[Bus-Transfer-Switch]] is in **OFF** |
