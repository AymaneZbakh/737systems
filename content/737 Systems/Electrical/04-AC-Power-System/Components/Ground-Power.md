# 🔧 Ground Power

> [!abstract] Overview
> External Ground Power provides up to 90 KVA through a dedicated receptacle, quality-checked and switched in by the [[BPCU]].

## 📋 Key Facts

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Output | 90 KVA |
| Receptacle rating | 115/200V, 400Hz |

## 💡 GRD POWER AVAILABLE Light

> [!note] Flight deck indication
> - Illuminates whenever ground power is available **and** meets the quality criteria
> - This is the **only light left illuminated** after a complete aircraft power-down
> - Always bright — ignores the master dim/bright switch (see [[BPCU]])
