# 🛡️ Cabin Pressure Relief System

🔗 [[Home - Air Systems]] | [[03-Pressurisation/Pressurisation System|Pressurisation]]

> [!abstract] Overview
> A failsafe system that protects the aircraft from structural damage due to overpressure or negative pressure if the pressurisation control system fails.

---

## 📋 Relief Valve Summary

| Component | Qty | Opens At | Purpose |
|-----------|-----|---------|---------|
| Positive Pressure Relief Valves | **2** | **9.1 psi** | Prevent structural overpressure |
| Negative Relief Valve | **1** | **−1.0 psi** | Prevent structural damage during emergency decompression |

> [!warning] Positive Relief Valves
> Two positive pressure relief valves set to open at **9.1 psi**. This is a hard structural protection — the normal operating maximum is 8.35 psi (>FL370), leaving a safety margin.

> [!warning] Negative Relief Valve
> Opens at **−1.0 psi** to prevent possible structural limit exceedance during emergency decompression (outside pressure briefly higher than cabin).

---

## ⚙️ Cargo Compartment Pressure Equalisation

| Component | Function |
|-----------|----------|
| Pressure Equalisation Valves | Located in cargo compartments — keep equal pressure between cargo and cabin sections |
| Blowout Panels (FWD) | **2×** — quickly equalise pressures between cabin and cargo in rapid decompression |
| Blowout Panels (AFT) | **3×** — same function aft |

> [!note] Cargo Sliding Carpet (Certain Models)
> On aeroplanes fitted with cargo sliding carpet, the carpet must be **extended all the way forward** for blowout panels to work properly in case of decompression.
