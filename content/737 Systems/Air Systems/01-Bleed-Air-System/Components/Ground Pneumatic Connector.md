# 🔌 Ground Pneumatic Connector

🔗 [[Home - Air Systems]] | [[01-Bleed-Air-System/Bleed Air System|Bleed Air System]]

> [!abstract] Overview
> Allows engine start using an external pneumatic source when the APU is inoperative. Connects to the **right side** of the bleed manifold.

---

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Purpose | Engine start with APU INOP |
| Connection point | Right side of bleed manifold |
| Max pressure | **60 psi** |
| Max temperature | **232°C / 450°F** |

---

## ⚠️ Procedures & Precautions

> [!warning] Do Not Confuse
> The Ground Pneumatic Connector is **not** the same as the [[Ground Conditioned Air Connector|Ground Conditioned Air Connector]]. Different connectors, different purposes.

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Battery power | Must be **ON** |
| PACK switches | Must be **OFF** (ensures pack valves closed during ground start) |
| Temperature/pressure | External source must remain within placard limits |
