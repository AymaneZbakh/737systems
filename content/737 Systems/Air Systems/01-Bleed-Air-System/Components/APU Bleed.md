# ⚙️ APU Bleed

🔗 [[Home - Air Systems]] | [[01-Bleed-Air-System/Bleed Air System|Bleed Air System]]

> [!abstract] Overview
> The APU can supply bleed air to one or both packs on the ground, and to one pack in flight, up to defined altitude limits.

---

## 📋 Capability

| Mode | Max Altitude |
|------|-------------|
| Bleed air only | **17,000 ft** |
| Bleed air + Electrical load | **10,000 ft** |
| Packs supplied on ground | **2 packs** |
| Packs supplied in flight | **1 pack** |

---

## ⚙️ Protection Components

| Component | Function |
|-----------|----------|
| APU Check Valve | Prevents engine bleed air from back-pressuring the APU |
| APU Pressure Relief Valve | Opens at **75 psi** to protect ducts from over-pressure |

---

## ⚠️ APU + Engine Interaction

> [!warning] Back-Pressure Risk
> APU bleed air may back-pressure the 9th stage engine bleed valve when engines run at idle thrust — this causes the 9th stage valve to close. Always operate engines at idle thrust when APU bleed is in use alongside engine bleed (see [[Components/PRSOV|DUAL BLEED]] condition).
