# 🌡️ Precooler & Precooler Control Valve

🔗 [[Home - Air Systems]] | [[01-Bleed-Air-System/Bleed Air System|Bleed Air System]] | [[PRSOV|← PRSOV]]

> [!abstract] Overview
> The Precooler is an air-to-air heat exchanger that reduces bleed air temperature before it enters the ducts. The Precooler Control Valve (PCV) regulates fan air cooling flow.

---

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Type | Air-to-air heat exchanger |
| Cooling source | Regulated fan air supply |
| Target temp range (downstream) | **199°C–229°C / 390–440°F** |
| Overheat action | PRSOV closes → BLEED TRIP-OFF |

---

## ⚙️ Precooler Control Valve (PCV)

| Condition | PCV Action |
|-----------|-----------|
| Normal operation | Modulates to maintain temp 199–229°C |
| Cannot cool sufficiently | PRSOV closes, BLEED TRIP-OFF illuminates |
| On ground with Wing Anti-Ice ON | PCV **fully opens** — max cooling to protect wing leading edges |

> [!note] Wing Anti-Ice on Ground
> With wing anti-ice ON on the ground, there is insufficient outside airflow cooling, so the PCV fully opens to maximise precooler effectiveness and prevent structural overheat of the leading edges.
