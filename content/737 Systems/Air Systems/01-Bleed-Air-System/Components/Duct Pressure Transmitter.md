# 📊 Duct Pressure Transmitter

🔗 [[Home - Air Systems]] | [[01-Bleed-Air-System/Bleed Air System|Bleed Air System]]

> [!abstract] Overview
> Each side of the bleed air manifold has its own Duct Pressure Transmitter, sending pressure indications to the air conditioning overhead panel.

---

## 📋 Pressure Reference Values

| Phase | Minimum Duct Pressure | Action if Below |
|-------|-----------------------|----------------|
| T/O, Climb, Cruise | **18 psi** | Maintenance required |
| Just after top of descent | **10 psi** | Maintenance required |
| Cross-bleed start (operating engine) | **30 psi** | Increase thrust to achieve |
| High pressure (any source) | **> 50 psi** | Maintenance required |
| External pneumatic max | **60 psi** | Placard limit |

---

## 🔢 Duct Pressure Split

> [!note] Duct Pressure Split is Normal
> The FCOM states **no limitations** on duct pressure split or minimum duct pressure differential. A split is typically caused by the L/R crossover from 9th to 5th stage occurring at different times. If within normal N1 vs altitude graphs, no maintenance action needed.

| Investigation Tool | Description |
|-------------------|-------------|
| Bleed Sheet | Fill in ALT, N1, Duct Press for each side |
| AMM Graph | "Duct Pressure Versus N1" at sea level, 10K, 22K, 31K, 37K, 41K ft |
