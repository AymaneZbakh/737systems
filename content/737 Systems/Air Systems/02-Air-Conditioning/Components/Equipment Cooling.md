# 🖥️ Equipment Cooling

🔗 [[Home - Air Systems]] | [[02-Air-Conditioning/Air Conditioning System|Air Conditioning]]

> [!abstract] Overview
> The Equipment Cooling system removes heat from the Display Units, CDUs, Central Pedestal, and E&E compartment using dedicated Supply and Exhaust fan systems.

---

## ⚙️ System Components

| Component | Function |
|-----------|----------|
| Supply Fans (NORM + ALTN) | Blow filtered air into electronic equipment |
| Exhaust Fans (NORM + ALTN) | Draw hot air out from electronic equipment |
| HEPA Filter | Filters supply air |
| Overboard Exhaust Valve | Routes exhaust overboard (low alt/ground) or to FWD cargo (in-flight) |

> [!info] Only One Fan Per System at a Time
> Both Supply and Exhaust systems have NORM and ALTN fans, but only one fan operates per system at a time. All fans are powered by **115V AC**.

---

## 🔀 Exhaust Routing

| Condition | Exhaust Goes To |
|-----------|----------------|
| Cabin diff press < 2 psi (low alt/ground) | **Overboard** via Overboard Exhaust Valve |
| In-flight (pressurised) | Overboard exhaust valve **closes** → air routed under FWD cargo floor (warms compartment) |

---

## 🔥 Smoke Removal Mode

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| R Recirculation Fan switched OFF **+** Either PACK in HIGH | Overboard Exhaust Valve signals **OPEN** |
| FWD Cargo Fire Warning (certain models) | Smoke removal mode activated automatically |

---

## 💡 OFF Light

> [!warning] Equipment Cooling OFF Light
> Illuminates when there is **insufficient airflow** through the system.
> - On the ground: a horn in the nose wheel well sounds to attract attention.
> - Selecting the alternate fan should restore airflow and extinguish the OFF light within ~5 seconds.
> - Disruption of equipment cooling can **signal impending pressurisation problems**.
