# 🔌 Ground Conditioned Air Connector

🔗 [[Home - Air Systems]] | [[02-Air-Conditioning/Air Conditioning System|Air Conditioning]]

> [!abstract] Overview
> An external source of conditioned air may be connected on the ground to supply the air conditioning system. Air is supplied **directly to the mix manifold**.

---

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Supply point | Directly to **mix manifold** |
| Use case | Ground air conditioning without running packs |
| PACKs when connected | Must be **OFF** |

> [!warning] Turn PACKs OFF When Using Ground Conditioned Air
> The cooling PACK **cannot control air temperature** when the ground air source is connected. Operating both simultaneously **can damage equipment**.

> [!note] Not the Same as Ground Pneumatic
> Do not confuse with the [[01-Bleed-Air-System/Components/Ground Pneumatic Connector|Ground Pneumatic Connector]] (used for engine start). Different connector, different location, different purpose.
