# 🌀 Ram Air System

🔗 [[Home - Air Systems]] | [[02-Air-Conditioning/Air Conditioning System|Air Conditioning]] | [[Packs|← Packs]]

> [!abstract] Overview
> The Ram Air system provides cooling airflow through the pack heat exchangers. Ram air inlet doors modulate between open and closed depending on cooling demand and flight conditions.

---

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Purpose | Cooling for Primary & Secondary Heat Exchangers |
| Ground cooling | ACM drives an impeller (ram air fan) to pull air through HX |
| In-flight | Ram air fan is **bypassed** — natural airflow used |
| Pack switched OFF | Ram air door **closes** (reduces drag) |

---

## ⚙️ Ram Air Door Behaviour

| Condition | Door Position |
|-----------|-------------|
| Ground / slow flight (flaps not fully retracted) | **Full open** — maximum cooling |
| Normal in-flight | Modulates based on cooling requirement |
| Pack OFF | Closes |

> [!warning] RAM DOOR FULL OPEN Light
> Illuminates on ground and during slow flight (flaps not fully retracted). If this light **stays on during flight with flaps up for extended periods**, it may indicate a **dirty or blocked heat exchanger**.

---

## 🛡️ Deflector Door

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Purpose | Protects ram air system from runway contaminants |
| Activation | Electrical, via air-ground safety sensor |
| When active | Deflects debris away from ram air inlet on ground |

---

## 💧 Water Injection

> [!info] Efficiency Enhancement
> Water collected downstream of the ACM is sprayed into the ram air to increase heat exchanger efficiency — effectively evaporative cooling.
