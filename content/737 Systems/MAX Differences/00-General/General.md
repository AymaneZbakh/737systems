# ✈ MAX-8 — General / Airplane Overview
🔗 [[Home - MAX Differences]]

> [!abstract] Overview
> The 737 MAX-8 is the fourth generation of the B737, succeeding the 737 NG. It is based on the NG airframe with a new LEAP-1B engine, aerodynamic refinements, and updated avionics. The aircraft does **not** require a special type rating — it is certified as part of the 737 family.

## Weight Differences vs 737-800

> [!warning] Heavier aircraft
> The MAX-8 is approx. **3,000 kg heavier** than the 737-800, primarily due to the heavier LEAP-1B engines (+385 kg each) and structural reinforcement of landing gear struts, engine struts, fuselage, and wings.
> Strengthening also **raised** the weight limits by ~3,190 kg.

| Weight Limitation | MAX-8 | 737-800 | Δ |
|-------------------|-------|---------|---|
| Maximum Taxi Mass | 82,417 kg | 79,225 kg | +3,192 kg |
| MTOM | 82,190 kg | 78,999 kg | +3,191 kg |
| MLM | 69,308 kg | 66,360 kg | +2,948 kg |
| MZFM | 65,952 kg | 62,731 kg | +3,221 kg |

## AT (Advanced Technology) Winglets

> [!note] New winglet design
> The MAX replaces the blended winglet of the NG with a new **Advanced Technology (AT) winglet** — a two-part design combining a blended upper section with a downward-pointing vertical strake.

- Lower airfoil produces a lift vector **away from fuselage and slightly forward** → improved span-wise load distribution
- Increases effective span → reduces induced drag → increases lift
- Special materials/coatings create laminar flow over the winglet
- Fuel efficiency improvement: **+1.5–1.8%** vs current blended winglets

### AT Winglet Specifics

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Static dischargers | 6 total (4 on AT winglet + 2 on wing tip). NG has only 2 |
| Strobe lights | Now mounted at root of lower airfoil |
| Aft marker light | White light illuminating lower winglet portion (position light function) |

> [!warning] Reduced ground contact margins in roll
> - **737-800**: strikes ground (flap track fairing) at 5° pitch-up when roll is slightly over **14°**
> - **737-8**: strikes ground (lower wing tip) at 5° pitch-up when roll is around **10°**

## Airframe Changes (Summary)

### Fuselage / Tail Section
- Tail section **streamlined** for better airflow
- Tail cone **extended**
- Stabilisers **strengthened**
- Aft body vortex generators **removed**
- Cooling eductor **removed**
- New dual tail **strobe/position lights**
- (Option) 2-position tail-skid

### Dimensions vs 737-800

## Onboard Network System (ONS)

> [!info] New health monitoring system
> The ONS monitors hardware and software for faults and provides engine health monitoring and recommended maintenance actions.

- Maintenance crew can view system status and faults on **flight deck displays** (not E&E bay bite tests)
- Stores up to **75 hours** of flight data on the ONS onboard server — **100× more data** than the NG
- Maintenance personnel can connect **wirelessly** to the onboard system
- Parts of the ONS system may be **retrofitted to the NG**

### PSEU Change
- **PSEU** light has been replaced by **MAINT** light
