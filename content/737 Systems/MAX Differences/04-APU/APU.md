# 🔋 MAX-8 APU
🔗 [[Home - MAX Differences]] | [[Engines|← Engines]] | [[Flight Controls|Next: Flight Controls →]]

> [!abstract] Overview
> The 737 MAX features a new APU inlet door (similar to the 787) and an updated Honeywell APU unit. APU start/shutdown procedures remain unchanged.

---

## APU Changes vs NG

| Feature | NG | MAX-8 |
|---------|----|-------|
| APU unit | Honeywell 131-9[B] | **Honeywell 131-9 (series 41)** |
| Starting reliability | Reference | **Improved** |
| Cooling eductor | Present | **Removed** |
| Cooling routing | Via eductor | Via **APU inlet door** |
| APU EGT indicator | On flight deck | **Removed** |
| APU MAINT light | On flight deck | **Removed** |
| APU start/shutdown | Standard sequence | **Unchanged** |

---

## APU Door

> [!note] New 3-position door
> The APU has a new inlet door similar to the 787. This door handles both cooling and APU air inlet.

| Position | Angle |
|---------|-------|
| Closed | 0° |
| Flight open | Approx. 17° |
| Ground open | Approx. 45° |

**Automatic operation when APU running:**
- At lift-off: door moves from Ground open → Flight open
- At touchdown: door moves from Flight open → Ground open

---

## DOOR Light (New indication)

> [!warning] DOOR light — new on MAX
> A new indication related to the APU door.

The DOOR light illuminates after **165 seconds** when:
- The APU door is **not in commanded position** while in flight (fuel burn penalty: **2.4%**)
- APU door remains open after APU shutdown
