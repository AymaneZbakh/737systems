# 🛫 MAX-8 Flaps & Slats
🔗 [[Home - MAX Differences]] | [[Spoilers|← Spoilers]] | [[Landing Gear|Next: Landing Gear →]]

> [!abstract] Overview
> The flaps and slats sequencing on the MAX-8 is identical to the Short Field Performance (SFP) configuration on the NG. The only notable difference is the F40 VFE speed increase.

---

## Differences vs NG

| Parameter | NG-800 | MAX-8 |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Flap/slat sequencing | Standard | Identical to NG **Short Field Performance** |
| F40 VFE | 162 KIAS | **166 KIAS** (+4 kts) |

---

## Interaction with Spoilers

The FBW spoiler system interacts with flap position for the **Landing Attitude Modifier (LAM)** and other functions. See [[Spoilers]] for full details.

| Flap Range | Spoiler Interaction |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Flaps 15–30 | LAM active — glide path correction |
| Flaps 30–40 | LAM active — pitch angle increase mode |
| Flaps 40 | Speedbrakes **should NOT** be extended |
