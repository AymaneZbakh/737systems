# 🌬 NGS — Deactivated Variant

> [!abstract] Overview
> On a subset of the fleet, the NGS hardware is physically present but not activated.

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Tail Range | NGS State |
|---|---|
| CN-RGE – CN-RGW, CN-ROS – CN-ROZ | Installed and **active** |
| CN-RNP – CN-ROR | **Partially installed and deactivated** |

> [!note] Operational Impact
> On deactivated-variant aircraft, center tank flammability reduction relies solely on ignition-source elimination — there is no active inerting. This is consistent with MEL guidance that treats NGS inoperative as dispatchable.
