# 🔌 APU Fuel Feed

> [!abstract] Overview
> APU fuel supply source depends on whether AC fuel pumps are running.

## 📋 Supply Logic

| Condition | APU Fuel Source |
|---|---|
| AC fuel pumps operating | Left side of the fuel manifold |
| AC fuel pumps **not** operating | Suction fed from **Main Tank No. 1** |

> [!info] See Also
> [[../../03-Fuel-Crossfeed-and-Shutoff/Components/APU-Fuel-Shutoff-Valve|APU Fuel Shutoff Valve]] governs the shutoff side of APU fuel supply.
