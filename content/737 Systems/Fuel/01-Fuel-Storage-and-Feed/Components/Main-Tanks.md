# 🛢 Main Tanks (No. 1 / No. 2)

> [!abstract] Overview
> Main Tank No. 1 and No. 2 are integral with the wing structure, each divided into bays by wing ribs, with a check valve at rib 8 controlling inboard fuel migration.

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Location | Integral with wing box structure |
| Capacity (each) | 4,875 L / 3,915 kg |
| Structure | Divided into bays by wing ribs |
| Rib 8 check valve | Allows flow **inboard only** (not outboard) |
| Associated surge tank | [[Surge-Tanks\|1 per main tank, 107 kg / 235 lb]] |
| Water scavenge | [[../../02-Fuel-Pumps-and-Suction-Feed/Overview\|Water scavenge ejector pumps]] remove water from low points |

> [!note] Water Scavenge Ejector Pumps
> Present in each tank to remove water from low points and prevent corrosion. They have **no rotating parts** — motive flow from the fuel boost pumps creates the suction needed to operate them.
