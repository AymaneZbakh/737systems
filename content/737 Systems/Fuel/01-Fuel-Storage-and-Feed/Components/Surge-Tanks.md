# 🛢 Surge Tanks

> [!abstract] Overview
> Each main tank has one surge tank that collects overflow fuel and drains it back to its respective main tank. If a surge tank fills, fuel drains overboard through the vent scoop.

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Quantity | 1 per main tank (2 total) |
| Capacity (each) | 107 kg / 235 lb |
| Function | Collects & drains overflow fuel back to respective main tank |
| Overflow condition | Fuel may drain out the vent scoop onto the ground |

> [!note] When Overboard Drainage Happens
> Most likely when tanks are **full** and fuel temperature increases, expanding fuel volume beyond surge tank capacity.
