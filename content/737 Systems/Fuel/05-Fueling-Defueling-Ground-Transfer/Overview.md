# 🛩 05 — Fueling, Defueling & Ground Transfer

> [!abstract] Overview
> All fueling, defueling, and tank-to-tank ground transfer is performed from a single-point pressure fueling station on the right wing leading edge, controlled by the refueling panel and gated by the manual defueling valve.

## 🛩 Refueling Panel

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Location | Leading edge of right wing |
| Function | Single-point refuel & defuel of all tanks; fuel transfer (with defueling valve open) |
| Power sources | External Ground Power (bus-connected or not) · Any AC source on the buses · Battery Power (battery switch ON) |

See [[Components/Refueling-Panel|Refueling Panel]] for full detail.

## 🔧 Defueling Methods

| Method | Mechanism | Eligible Tanks | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pressure defueling | Fuel pump pressure | **Any tank** | Standard method |
| Suction defueling | Fuel truck suction pump | **Main Tank No. 2 only** | Use only when aircraft electrical power is unavailable |

> [!warning] Suction Defueling Restriction
> Suction defueling should be used **only** when electrical power on the aircraft is unavailable, and is only valid for **Main Tank No. 2**.
