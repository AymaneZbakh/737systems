# 🖥 Test Gages & Fueling Panel

> [!abstract] Overview
> Located at the right wing leading edge, this panel exists in two hardware variants across the fleet: an analog round-gauge version and a digital-readout version.

## 📋 Fueling Indication Test Switch (spring-loaded to OFF)

| Position | Function |
|---|---|
| TEST GAGES | Checks operation of the fuel quantity indicators |
| FUEL DOOR SWITCH BYPASS | Energizes the fueling panel if the [[Refueling-Power-Control-Relay\|refueling power control relay]] fails |

## 🆚 Panel Variants

| Feature | Analog Panel (CN-RGV, ROH, ROJ) | Digital Panel (CN-RGE–RGN, RGW–ROE, ROK–ROZ) |
|---|---|---|
| Fuel quantity display | Round dial gauge (e.g. "2080 KG") | 5-digit numeric readout |
| Valve Position Lights | Present (OPEN/CLOSED per tank) | Present (OPEN/CLOSED per tank) |
| Fuel Quantity Selector | Not applicable | Rotary selector — sets desired total quantity |
