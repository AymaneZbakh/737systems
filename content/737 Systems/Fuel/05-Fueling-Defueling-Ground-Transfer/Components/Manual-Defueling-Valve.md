# 🔧 Manual Defueling Valve

> [!abstract] Overview
> Interconnects the engine feed system and the fueling station, opened to enable defueling and tank-to-tank ground transfer.

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Location | Leading edge of right wing, inboard of the refueling station / outboard of Engine No. 2 |
| Operation | Pull the defueling valve handle **down** |
| Open | Interconnects engine feed system and fueling station — enables defueling and ground transfer |
| Closed | Isolates engine feed system from fueling station |

## 🔧 Defueling Methods

| Method | Uses | Eligible Tanks | Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pressure defueling | Fuel pump pressure | Any tank | Standard |
| Suction defueling | Fuel truck's suction pump | **Main Tank No. 2 only** | Use only when aircraft electrical power is unavailable |

> [!warning] Suction Defueling
> Only Main Tank No. 2 can be suction defueled, and it should be used **only** when electrical power on the aircraft is unavailable.
