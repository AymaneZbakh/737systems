# 🛩 Refueling Panel

> [!abstract] Overview
> Single-point refuel/defuel panel located on the leading edge of the right wing, capable of powering itself from ground power, aircraft AC power, or battery power alone.

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Location | Leading edge of right wing |
| Function | Single-point refuel and defuel of all tanks |
| Fuel transfer | Possible with the [[Manual-Defueling-Valve\|defueling valve]] open |

## 🔌 Power Sources

| Source | Condition |
|---|---|
| External Ground Power | Whether or not connected to the buses |
| Any AC power source | Connected to the buses |
| Battery Power | Battery switch ON |
