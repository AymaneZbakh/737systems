# 🔧 Fueling Valves

> [!abstract] Overview
> Solenoid-operated valves that admit fuel to each tank during refueling, with a mechanical solenoid override and switch-controlled energizing.

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Fueling Valve Switches — OPEN | Energizes the fueling valve in the related tank |
| Fueling Valve Switches — CLOSED | De-energizes the fueling valve in the related tank |
| Fueling Valves | With battery switch ON and refueling door open, fuel pressure opens the valve |
| Solenoid Override | Mechanically opens the solenoid-operated valve; valve opens if fuel pressure is available |
| Fueling Receptacle | Hose connection point for single-point fueling |

## 💡 Fueling VALVE POSITION Lights

| State | Condition |
|---|---|
| Extinguished | Switch OPEN **and** related tank is full — or — switch is CLOSED |
| Illuminated (blue) | Switch OPEN **and** related tank is **not** full |
