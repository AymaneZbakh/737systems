# ⚙️ Main Tank Fuel Pumps

> [!abstract] Overview
> Two AC-powered pumps per main tank, cooled and lubricated by fuel passing through them, producing lower output pressure than the center tank pumps so that center tank fuel is used first.

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Pumps per tank | 2 |
| Output pressure | 10 psi |
| Flow rate | 9,071 kg/h (20,000 lb/h) |
| Power source | Transfer buses (AC) |
| Cooling/lubrication | By fuel flow through the pump |
| Discharge check valve | Prevents fuel flow from engine feed manifold back to the tank |

## 🚨 LOW PRESSURE Light

> [!warning] Main Fuel Pump LOW PRESSURE
> - Illuminates when fuel pump output pressure is **< 4 psi**, or the Main Fuel Pump switch is **OFF**
> - **One** light illuminated → Master Caution & FUEL annunciator on **RECALL**
> - **Both** lights illuminated in one tank → Master Caution & FUEL annunciator illuminate directly

> [!note] Flickering
> LOW PRESSURE lights may flicker when tank quantity is low and the airplane is in a climb, descent, or on the ground with a nose-down attitude.
