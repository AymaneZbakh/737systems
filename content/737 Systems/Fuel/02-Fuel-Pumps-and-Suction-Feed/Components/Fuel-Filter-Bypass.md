# 🚨 Fuel Filter Bypass

> [!abstract] Overview
> Warns of an impending fuel filter bypass caused by a contaminated filter, with a direct risk to engine operation.

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Light | Condition |
|---|---|
| FILTER BYPASS (amber) | Impending fuel filter bypass due to a contaminated filter |
| Extinguished | Fuel filter operating normally |

> [!warning] Engine Impact
> The affected engine **may run erratically or flame out** if the filter bypasses under contamination.
