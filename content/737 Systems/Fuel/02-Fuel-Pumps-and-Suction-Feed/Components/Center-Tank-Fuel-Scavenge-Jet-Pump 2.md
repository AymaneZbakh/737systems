# ⚙️ Center Tank Fuel Scavenge Jet Pump

> [!abstract] Overview
> Recovers residual center tank fuel by transferring it into Main Tank No. 1, activating automatically partway through the flight and continuing for the remainder of the flight.

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Transfers to | Main Tank No. 1 |
| Transfer rate | 100–200 kg/h (220–440 lb/h) |
| Auto-activation condition 1 | Main Tank 1 Forward fuel pump switch **ON** |
| Auto-activation condition 2 | Main Tank 1 quantity ≈ **half full** (~1,990 kg / 4,300 lb) — float valve opens |
| Once started | Continues for the **remainder of the flight** |
| Moving parts | None — it is a jet pump with no rotating parts |

> [!note] Purpose
> Recovers fuel that would otherwise remain trapped and unusable in the center tank once boost pump pressure there is exhausted.
