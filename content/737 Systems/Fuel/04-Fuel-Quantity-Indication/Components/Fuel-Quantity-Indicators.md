# 🖥 Fuel Quantity Indicators

> [!abstract] Overview
> Displays total usable fuel quantity per tank in the flight deck (center upper display unit) and, during refueling, on the refuel panel itself.

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Display | Shows |
|---|---|
| Center upper display unit (flight deck) | Fuel quantity per tank, continuously |
| Refuel panel indicator | Fuel quantity per tank, during refuel operations |
| CN-RGW variant | Also displays **total fuel quantity** beneath individual tank quantities |

## 🎛 Fuel Quantity Selectors (Refuel Panel — Digital Variants)

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| Rotate selector | Sets the desired total fuel quantity for the related tank (drives auto shutoff during fueling) |

> [!note] Variant Differences
> See [[../../Quick-Reference/Variant Comparison|Variant Comparison]] — analog-dial refuel panels (e.g. CN-RGV, ROH, ROJ) versus digital refuel panels with quantity selectors (e.g. CN-RGE–RGN, RGW–ROE, ROK–ROZ).
