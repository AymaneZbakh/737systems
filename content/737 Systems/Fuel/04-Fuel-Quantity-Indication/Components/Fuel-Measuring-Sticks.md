# 📏 Fuel Measuring Sticks

> [!abstract] Overview
> A manual backup method to cross-check fuel quantity or weight against the FQIS reading, using magnetic float-latching sticks withdrawn from each tank.

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Sticks per main tank | 6 |
| Sticks in center tank | 4 |
| Method | Withdraw stick, latch magnetically to internal float |
| Reading | Fuel depth read where the stick passes through the wing skin |
| Purpose | Compare against FQIS-indicated fuel weight/quantity |
