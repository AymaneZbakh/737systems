# 🔋 Fuel Shutoff Battery

> [!abstract] Overview
> A dedicated battery, recharged whenever not in use, that guarantees the fuel system always retains the power needed to close critical shutoff valves — independent of the main aircraft battery bus state.

> [!warning] Safety-Critical
> This is a **backup power path** — its entire purpose is to ensure fire-related fuel shutoff commands succeed even under a primary electrical failure.
