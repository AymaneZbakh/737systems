# 🔒 Spar Fuel Shutoff Valve

> [!abstract] Overview
> Located at the engine-mounting wing station, DC motor operated from the hot battery bus, closing on fire switch or start lever CUTOFF commands.

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Location | Engine-mounting wing station |
| Actuation | DC motor |
| Primary power | HOT BAT BUS |
| Backup power | [[Fuel-Shutoff-Battery\|Fuel Shutoff Battery]] |
| Closes when | Engine fire switch pulled, or engine start lever placed to CUTOFF |

> [!note] Guaranteed Closure
> The Fuel Shutoff Battery ensures the fuel system **always has power to close** this valve, even if HOT BAT BUS power is lost.
