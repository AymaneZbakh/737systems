# 🎮 Crossfeed Valve

> [!abstract] Overview
> Butterfly-type valve, DC motor operated from the battery bus, connecting the left and right fuel manifolds so either engine can draw fuel from any tank.

## 📋 Quick Facts

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Butterfly |
| Actuation | DC motor |
| Power | Battery (BAT) Bus |
| Function | Interconnects engine No.1 / No.2 fuel manifolds |

## 💡 CROSSFEED VALVE OPEN Light (blue)

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Extinguished | Valve closed |
| Illuminated — bright | Valve in transit, **or** valve position disagrees with CROSSFEED selector |
| Illuminated — dim | Valve open |

## 🎛 CROSSFEED Selector

| Position | Effect |
|---|---|
| Closed | Isolates engine No.1 and No.2 fuel feed lines |
| Open | Connects engine No.1 and No.2 fuel feed lines |

> [!warning] Fuel Balancing Caution
> Do **not** open the crossfeed immediately after an in-flight engine shutdown — it can mask a fuel leak. Follow the FCOM Supplementary Procedure for fuel balancing.
